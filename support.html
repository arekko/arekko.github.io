Support

Need help, found a bug, or have a feature request? Email support@liftyx.app. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

What are the requirements?

Liftyx runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. Liftyx has no accounts and no cloud sync; your workouts, routines, and statistics never leave your iPhone unless you enable usage data (see below) — which never includes workout contents.

How do I turn off anonymous usage data?

Open Settings in the app, then Privacy, and switch off "Share usage data". This immediately disables both usage analytics and crash reporting. You can turn it back on at any time.

How do I delete my data?

  • Delete an individual completed workout from the History tab.
  • To remove everything, open Settings in the app, tap "Manage workouts", and choose "Reset app data". This deletes all workouts, routines, and custom exercises, then restores the built-in defaults.
  • Deleting the app from your device removes all of its data as well.

How do I switch between kg and lbs?

Open Settings in the app and change "Weight units". All values update everywhere in the app.

Can I start a workout from a saved routine?

Yes. Open the Routines tab, pick a routine, and start it. When you finish, you can update the routine or save the session as a new one.

Does Liftyx need an internet connection?

No. The app works fully offline. Only the optional anonymous usage and crash reports use the network, and you can turn those off in Settings.

How do I report a crash or a bug?

Email support@liftyx.app with a short description and, if possible, the steps to reproduce it. Your anonymous crash reports (if enabled) help us fix issues faster.
